## 5. Findings that change authority interpretation

1. **The historical development line is not equivalent to a single merged product baseline.** Several foundational mechanism PRs (#1–#5, #8–#15) were merged into `main`, but other relevant PRs remain open/unmerged (#6, #16, #29, #95, #97), were closed without merge (#7, #20, #118), or were merged only into feature/integration branches (#101, #121).
2. **Duplicate, sibling and rebased PRs must not be double-counted.** #7 shares #6's exact head; #8 is a distinct merged sibling/alternative that diverges from #6 at the PR #5 merge base, not a descendant. #20 was closed unmerged; #21 is the merged rebased successor.
3. **Test PRs are not interchangeable with accepted capabilities.** #99 merged into main, while #101 merged into `feat/jarvis-affective-state`; #107/#109/#111 remain open; #118 closed unmerged. Their existence or titles do not prove integrated runtime behavior.
4. **The broad PR #97 candidate is not an accepted implementation baseline.** Its 159-path source audit and limitations are already recorded in DF-02 §9. PR #156 is a later unmerged integration candidate, and PR #176's correction branch has its own ancestry divergence and limits. No wholesale adoption or selective port is authorized.
5. **The PR graph is branched.** Base branch names and base SHAs above are material. A PR's declared base branch may have moved since the recorded base SHA, and branch-scoped merge evidence cannot be generalized to main.
6. **A merged change is historical integration evidence, not proof of present capability.** Current source must be inspected at the selected baseline, and capability claims require relevant tests/evaluation evidence on that exact revision.
